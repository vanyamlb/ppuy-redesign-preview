Who we are

A community for people, culture, and action.

Ukrainians of Northern Ostrobothnia is a community and a voice for Ukrainians in the region. The association was founded in January 2023 following volunteer work in Oulu.

Learn more→
01

Culture

Ukrainian traditions, language, and contemporary culture are at the heart of our community.

02

Life in Finland

We help Ukrainians get to know local culture and build everyday life in Finland.

03

Support for Ukraine

Together with our partners, we collect and send aid supplies to Ukraine.
