What we do

Action with purpose

We connect people, celebrate Ukrainian culture, and organize support for Ukraine.

01

Ukrainian Encounters

The 2026 gatherings have explored Ukrainian songs, history and visual art. Music and conversation bring Ukrainians and Finnish friends together.

02

A space for children

The Ukrainian language Lukuleikit club meets at KOTILO for play, reading, drawing and storytelling.

03

Everyday connections

A book club, family outings and shared hikes make room for friendship and exploring northern nature.

04

Wellbeing and peer support

Women’s circles create a small group setting for conversations about experiences and relationships. Current meetings appear on the events page.

05

Standing with Ukraine

The association holds regular gatherings in central Oulu in support of Ukraine and shares Ukrainian culture at public events.

06

Humanitarian support

Together with Finnish partners, we collect and deliver aid supplies to Ukraine. Earlier Help Center work also helped people arriving in Finland with practical needs.

PPUY / OULU

Regular gatherings

The association gathers in central Oulu on Sundays from 14:00 to 14:30 to support Ukraine. The route runs from the Toripolliisi statue past Valkea to the Odessa sign. Check the WhatsApp group for current details.
